AI Logistics Agent

An intelligent Google Sheets assistant that helps manage a 204-school tablet rollout across Sarawak by answering questions about deadlines, overdue tasks, and contacts instantly.

Explore the Project See the Impact

The Problem

All over Sarawak, many schools don't have opportunities for students to catch up in their studies. Some of them can't even read or write. There are many people involved in this project, such as teachers, delivery drivers, and volunteers.

The problems arise not just during the process of delivering the tablets but before delivering the tablets. These tablets are loaded with educational materials from the Kayam App to help these students, but managing a 204-school logistics rollout is complex.

How can we make it easy for those who are involved in the project to know what is overdue and what the upcoming deadlines are?

The Process

Mr Chong, my teacher in Chumbaka, gave an idea to develop an AI agent inside Google Sheets to answer questions based on what the users ask. The AI agent is basically like a human assistant, except it has everything stored in the database.

The Tool: Google Sheets

Used Google Sheets because of prior experience working with it, leveraging Google Apps Script and Python to build the solution.

Initial Challenge

At the start, wasn't even sure it would work. Eventually managed to connect ChatGPT to Google Sheets using Google Apps Script by coding it to respond to simple questions before reading sheet data.

Scaling Issue: 1,000+ Rows

Getting the AI to read all data with over a thousand rows was like giving someone a 1,000-page book and asking a question. It was too slow and too costly every single time.

Vector Database Solution

Instead of reading the whole book, the AI uses a smart index to flip to the exact paragraph it needs instantly. A vector database searches using numerical codes rather than exact words. Data syncs to the cloud manually or every 1–2 hours.

Future Improvements

Planned enhancements to make the AI agent even more powerful and accessible for everyone involved in the project.

Automated Notifications

Have a bar which can track dates and notify what tasks are overdue without requiring input. Possibly connect through email, WhatsApp, or Telegram.

Suggested Questions

In the AI sidebar, include suggested questions to ask — helping users discover what the agent can do.

Sync Progress Bar

Have a progress bar to show how far the syncing is for the data to reach the database in the cloud.

Enhanced UI

Make the UI of the AI look nicer, so that the user feels more excited to use it.

Mobile Availability

Make this AI feature available on phones. Mobile phones are more portable and easier to bring around and update immediately rather than a computer.

Seamless Cloud Sync

Improve the cloud sync experience so data is always up-to-date and available across all devices in real time.
